## A. HEADLINE: One real conflation — CCO transit-served distance for bus corridors

**The renderer collapses the CCO's two distinct radii into one.** At [pdf-export.ts:3303](artifacts/tis-api-server/src/lib/pdf-export.ts:3303) the renderer says:

> "Connected Communities Ordinance transit-served-location designation (½ mile of a CTA/Metra rail station entrance **or eligible high-frequency bus corridor**) drives by-right parking reductions…"

The ½-mile (2,640 ft) radius applies **only to CTA/Metra rail station entrances/exits**. **Bus corridors get a quarter-mile (1,320 ft) radius**, measured to the roadway centerline of select CTA/Pace corridors with ≤15-minute midday headways (per chicago.gov CCO Full Explainer and chicagocityscape's "Connected Communities" reference page). The renderer text reads as if ½ mile applies to both, which is incorrect. The IL spec at §1.2 actually gets this right ("within 2,640 ft / ½ mi of a CTA or Metra rail station entrance, plus eligible high-frequency bus corridors") — the regression is in the renderer.

A consultant reading the screening output and assuming a ½-mile bus-corridor buffer would over-claim transit-served eligibility on roughly 3× the actual buffer area and risk an embarrassing PRC kickoff. Fix the two-radius wording explicitly.

Everything else verified clean. The renderer's three landmark Illinois citations — **68 Ill. Adm. Code §1380.295** (PE digital seal), **CDOT TDM Guidelines v1.2 (February 5, 2024)**, and **O2025-0015577** (July 16, 2025 parking-mandate amendment, effective September 25, 2025) — are all correct, including verbatim dates and ordinance numbers.

## C. Verified-WRONG citations that need fixing

### C.1  CCO transit-served bus-corridor distance is ¼ mile, not ½ mile

**Renderer at [pdf-export.ts:3303](artifacts/tis-api-server/src/lib/pdf-export.ts:3303):**
> "Connected Communities Ordinance transit-served-location designation (½ mile of a CTA/Metra rail station entrance or eligible high-frequency bus corridor) drives by-right parking reductions…"

This conflates two distinct CCO buffer distances:

| Trigger | Distance | Renderer? |
|---|---|---|
| CTA / Metra rail station entrance or exit | **2,640 ft (½ mi)** | ✓ correct |
| Select CTA / Pace bus corridors (≤15-min midday headway) | **1,320 ft (¼ mi)**, to roadway centerline | ✗ renderer states ½ mi |

**Action:** rewrite the parenthetical to "(½ mile of a CTA/Metra rail station entrance or exit, OR ¼ mile of an eligible high-frequency CTA/Pace bus corridor measured to roadway centerline)." The same fix may be needed in any TDM-tier sub-renderer that inherits the same string (`renderTisIllinoisCdotWorksheet`, `renderTisIllinoisCdotAbbreviated` — verify both subrenderers since the wrong wording could be cloned).

### C.2  "BDE Ch. 17 non-motorized warrants" — wording mischaracterizes the chapter

**Renderer at [pdf-export.ts:3583](artifacts/tis-api-server/src/lib/pdf-export.ts:3583):**
> "Pedestrian and bicycle accommodations are evaluated against BDE Ch. 17 non-motorized warrants."

BDE Ch. 17 ("Bicycle and Pedestrian Accommodations") is **accommodations guidance**, not a warrants matrix. ILMUTCD has formal pedestrian signal warrants (Warrant 4) and school crossing warrants (Warrant 5); BDE Ch. 17 references those upstream documents but is itself a facility-selection chapter built on AASHTO 2012 + NCHRP. Soften to: "Pedestrian and bicycle accommodations follow the BDE Ch. 17 facility-selection criteria; pedestrian-signal-related warrants are evaluated under ILMUTCD." Minor — fixable in a one-line wording edit.

### C.3  IL spec is stale on CDOT TDM v1.1 → v1.2

The **spec** ([illinois-tis-spec.md §1.2 line 72, §10 open-question 7](REGIONAL-SPECS/illinois-tis-spec.md)) still treats v1.1 (June 2023) as the operative doc and lists "locate v1.2 / current edition if released since June 2023" as an open question. The renderer correctly uses v1.2. **Action:** update the spec to match the renderer (the renderer is right), not the other way around. This is a spec-vs-renderer drift, not a renderer error.

## F. Net assessment

The Illinois renderer is in noticeably better shape than the California, Georgia, or Florida modules. It cites the right primary documents (BLRS, BDE, D8 Appx. A, CDOT TDM v1.2, CCO, §1380.295, MYP FY 2026–2031), gets the correct ordinance numbers and effective dates for the 2025 CCO parking-mandate amendment, names the IDOT-CDOT MOU correctly, and avoids hard-pinning ITE / HCM editions. The one real error is the CCO bus-corridor radius (C.1) — fix that one line and the renderer is materially correct.

No supersession surprises in the Jan-2026-to-Jun-2026 window: §1380.295 is stable (last amended Jan 5, 2023), CDOT TDM v1.2 was the operative version at the cutoff and remains so, O2025-0015577 was confirmed as enacted and in effect since September 25, 2025, IDOT MYP FY 2026–2031 was rolled out on schedule. No Illinois analog to Georgia's HB 297 (agency dissolution) or California's AB 2553 (statute amendment).
